Reading this

Maintenance is roughly what keeps your weight where it is now. To lose weight, you eat somewhat below that — the two paces above trade off speed for how easy the deficit is to actually stick to. Gentle is generally the easier one to sustain without it taking over your life — a sensible place to start.

This is an estimate, not a prescription — it'll be off by a bit either way, and your actual number may need adjusting once you see how your weight responds over a couple of weeks.

What your calories are made of matters too

For weight loss, protein can be raised to 30% or more — it helps you stay full for longer while keeping total daily calories lower. For building muscle, aim for a higher protein intake around 30% too, with the rest split between carbs for training energy and fats for hormones.

Keep moving — just don't expect it to outrun your plate

Movement supports weight loss but it can't cancel out food that isn't supporting what you're trying to do — burning off a single biscuit can take a mile and a half of running, which isn't a realistic way to manage weight day to day. Keep moving. Just get the food side right too — one doesn't substitute for the other.
