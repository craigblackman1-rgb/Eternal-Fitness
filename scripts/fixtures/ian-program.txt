WORKOUT A: Warm-up — Circuit, 2 rounds, no rest between exercises: Forward Leg Swing 10 reps; Lateral Leg Swing 10; Marching On The Spot 2 min; Hip Circles 10; Shoulder Rolls & Arm Swings (LEFT shoulder only) 10; Trunk Rotation 10; Bodyweight Squat 10. Superset 2: Half-Kneeling Single Arm Row (LEFT arm only) 3x10 @ 13.5kg; Sissy Squat (supported) 3x8-10 bodyweight shallow range. Superset 3: Alternating Dumbbell Step Up 3x60 sec Level 3; Balance Ladder Drill 1-2 sets. Standalone — straight sets, rest 90 sec: Standing Pallof Press (both sides) 3x10 each side, light band. Superset 4 of 3: Band Single Arm Bicep Curl (LEFT) 3x12; Single Arm Dumbbell Overhead Tricep Extension (LEFT) 3x12; Single Arm Front Raise (LEFT) 3x12. Standalone: Dead Bug With Ball 3x10 each side. Cool-down same as A.
